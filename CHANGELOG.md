# Changelog

## 0.1.1 alpha

- Works with **any graphics card** on the PC (needs ArcSync 0.1.6+): AMD, NVIDIA, Intel or software H.264.
- Works on **any TV chip**: every video decoder is tried — low-latency hardware, other hardware, software — and vendor low-latency settings are retried without them if a decoder rejects them.
- The app tells the PC which codecs it can decode; devices without HEVC get H.264 automatically.
- "AMF encoder" setting renamed to "PC encoder".

## 0.1 alpha

First public alpha of ArcSync Cast TV, an add-on for [ArcSync](https://github.com/Rezerv-EM/ArcSync) 0.1.5+.
